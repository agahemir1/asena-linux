Every change applies without logging out.

## Appearance

Settings › Appearance:

- **Color scheme:** follow the system, light or dark. The Dark style button in the control center changes the same setting.
- **Accent color:** blue, teal, green, yellow, orange, red, pink, purple and slate. The color you pick reaches Asena’s apps, the folder icons and Flatpak apps together.
- **Transparency effects:** when on, the panel, menu, control center and notification panel look like frosted glass and blur what is behind them. When off, they are opaque and the blur is not loaded at all.
- **Text scale:** makes all interface text larger or smaller.
- **Background:** pick one of Asena’s ten wallpapers or your own picture, and choose how it is scaled.

## Desktop and panel

Settings › Desktop and panel:

- **Panel position:** bottom, top, left or right. On a vertical panel the icons stay upright and the clock switches to a narrow layout.
- **Application alignment:** apps at the start, center or end of the panel.
- **Panel size** and **icon size**.
- **Show on all monitors:** when off, the panel stays on one screen.
- **Menu type:** Odak (a single-column list, the default), Classic (categories on the left) or Grid. Pinned apps, search and shortcuts stay the same in all three.
- **Reset:** returns the panel and menu layout to the defaults without touching your other preferences.

The panel clock’s format (24 or 12 hour, with seconds if you like) lives in Settings › Date and time.

## Night light

The Night light button in the control center warms the colors of the screen.

## Accessibility

Settings › Accessibility has text scale and animations. When animations are off, the shell’s transitions stop and the compositor’s window animation plugin is not loaded.

## Wallpapers

Ten wallpapers ship in `/usr/share/backgrounds/asena/`; the default is Asena Night. All are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The [Story](/en/story/) page tells the motif behind each one.
