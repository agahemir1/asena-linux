The audit on 21 October turned up an unpleasant picture: every app crashed at start-up, and fifteen of them were mockups with no real data behind them. We withdrew the app report written the day before.

## What we did

The shared app framework was fixed. Files, Settings, Text Editor, Notes, Calendar, Clocks, Calculator, Photos, Media Player, Archive and Screenshot were rewritten against real data.

The five apps that were not ready (Terminal, Camera, Documents, Weather and Software) were taken out of the menu. Opened by hand, each shows a window saying it is not ready yet and what to use instead, if anything. Software was finished on 23 October and is back in the menu.

## The shell, too

The same day the shell surfaces were rebuilt to the Odak design: status icons, a single-column menu, the control center, a notification panel that slides in from the right, and desktop icons. Our own line icon set arrived.

## The rule

No fake data and no mockups that do not work are left in Asena. The screenshots on this site come from a real session too.
