This site is for anyone curious about Asena. On the [Desktop](/en/desktop/) page you can click your way around the shell, in [Apps](/en/apps/) you can look at the real windows of the twelve apps, and in [Story](/en/story/) you can read the legend behind the name and the motifs of the wallpapers.

## The images are real

Every screenshot on the site was taken from a real Asena session in the development virtual machine. Turkish pages show the Turkish interface, English pages the English one. The script that takes them lives in the repository, so they can be retaken with one command as the interface changes.

## What it does not have

No cookies, no visitor analytics and no fonts loaded from third parties. Search runs in your browser.

## Keeping up

Follow new posts through the [RSS feed](/en/news/rss.xml). When the first ISO is ready, we will announce it here.
